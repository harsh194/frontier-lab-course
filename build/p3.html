Paper: ThunderKittens — Notes from Zero

A beginner-friendly walkthrough of "ThunderKittens: Simple, Fast, and Adorable AI Kernels" — a framework that makes writing fast GPU kernels far less painful. These notes assume you have never written a GPU kernel or opened a CUDA file: they build the mental model from the ground up — what a GPU is, how it runs code, where the bottlenecks live — and only then read the paper.

This connects directly to the kernels and Flash-Attention material in the course track: ThunderKittens is about how the fast operations you've been learning about actually get written.
