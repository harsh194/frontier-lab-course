Paper: FlashAttention-4 — A Ground-Up Study Guide

A friendly, from-zero guide to making attention run fast on modern GPUs. You do not need to know anything about computer chips — the notes below build the whole picture (what a GPU is, where the bottlenecks live, and the core FlashAttention idea of never writing the giant attention matrix to slow memory) one step at a time, in plain English.

Source note

This study guide teaches the FlashAttention technique family, which is stable, well-established knowledge. The specific "FlashAttention-4" framing references a future-dated arXiv ID (2603.05451) and Blackwell-era hardware — treat author names and exact benchmark numbers as unverified and read the primary source before quoting them. The mechanism (tiling, online softmax, kernel fusion) is the real, durable content.
